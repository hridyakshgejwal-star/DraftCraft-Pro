# External compatibility fixtures

These unmodified minimal fixtures originate from Martin Vilcans’ MIT-licensed Screenplain project, not DraftCraft. Source: https://github.com/vilcans/screenplain/tree/abf0d0800ab0b9dfd5bbe780137641e746afbad4/tests/files . The accompanying LICENSE.txt applies to these fixtures.

They demonstrate interoperability with an independent Fountain/FDX/PDF writer. They do not establish that this build has been opened or tested in the Final Draft desktop application. Larger copyrighted screenplay examples from that repository are not redistributed.
