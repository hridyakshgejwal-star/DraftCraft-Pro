# DraftCraft Pro — Caret Animation

Open **DraftCraft Pro → Settings → Editor → Caret Animation**.

- **Smooth Caret Movement** turns animated movement on or off. New profiles start on; your existing saved On/Off choice is preserved.
- **Animation Speed:** Fast (60 ms), Normal (80 ms), or Slow (100 ms). Older custom speeds remain available.
- **Animation Style:** Ease-Out, Spring, or Linear. Spring is damped and does not bounce or overshoot.
- **Smooth Caret Blinking** fades the caret during idle blinking. Turn it off for a familiar stepped blink.
- **Caret Blink Speed** adjusts the blink cycle. Turn off **Blink caret after a pause** for a steady caret.
- Thin and wide line appearances and the existing optional character highlight remain available.

Text appears immediately. Motion redirects to the latest insertion position and settles without an animation queue. Nearby line moves can glide; wrapping, page changes, scrolling, zoom and large jumps reposition immediately.

The caret is steady while you type and begins blinking after a short pause. Selection, app switching, IME composition, reduced motion and high contrast use the normal native behavior. Bidirectional text retains its native caret affinity.

All editing continues in the original textarea. The visual caret does not change text, selections, shortcuts, undo/redo, pagination, project data or autosave. The settings persist independently from screenplay files.
